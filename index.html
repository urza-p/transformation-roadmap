What we added

We treated Schneider Electric's announced initiatives as evidence for capabilities, then made our sequencing logic explicit where the public record does not establish a direct dependency. In particular, we label the Open Talent Market to Industrial Copilot and product-data digitization to sustainability reporting links as our interpretation. We also distinguish a capability that scales from a single product that scales: Resource Advisor+ and EcoStruxure IT are separate products, so our Run phase describes the expansion of connected AI and sustainability capabilities. The dashboard is designed around leadership decisions, not a list of launches. It separates readiness and adoption signals from realized outcomes and surfaces the funding, architecture, and scale decisions that the RACI assigns to named roles.
